How HotSync works

1. LoadRead the MODIS and VIIRS CSV files from NASA FIRMS
2. CleanKeep vegetation fires, remove low confidence and factories
3. Make fairFind the VIIRS ÷ MODIS ratio on days both saw fires, convert MODIS
4. ShowMap, chart, calendar, table and CSV download
